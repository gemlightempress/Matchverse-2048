Privacy Policy

What Matchverse 2048 collects, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Overview

Matchverse: 2048 Number Merge is developed and published by Gemlight Empress. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. The game has no account system and does not ask for your email, contacts or location.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your level progress and stars, Crown Coins, statistics, settings (music, sound, vibration and reminders), the level in progress, Daily Treasure progress, your chosen display name and a random Player ID in the app's local storage on your device. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or using Reset Progress deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner during play, full-screen ads between levels, and optional reward videos you choose to watch. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model and app interactions. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice anytime from Settings → Ad Privacy Choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Google Play Games (optional)

You can sign in with Google Play Games to save your progress in the cloud and earn achievements, or play as a guest. When signed in, your Play Games profile name and an anonymous player ID are used by Google, and your game progress (levels, stars, Crown Coins, lives and achievements) is stored in your Google Play Games account. You can play without signing in.

Analytics and crash reports (Firebase)

The game uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played (for example which levels are started and completed) and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports, so we can fix problems. This data is collected by Google with an app instance ID and device information; it does not include your name or contact details. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Crown Coin packs are sold through Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled entirely by Google; we never see your card or payment information. We only receive a purchase confirmation so the Crown Coins can be added to your game.

Reminders

If Reminders is on in Settings, the game schedules notifications on your device (a daily Daily Treasure reminder and a reminder after a couple of days away). They are created and shown by your phone; no data is sent to us. Android asks for your permission first, and you can turn them off anytime in Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

Sharing

If you use the share buttons in Settings, the app opens Facebook, WhatsApp or your phone's share sheet with a short message and a link to the game. Nothing is shared unless you choose to.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here and the game will ask you to accept it again.
